One of the most frequently asked questions that is directed to the GFSB is “when will the treaty come into force?”.
Insofar as the GFSB has been able to establish, there is currently no information in the public domain about when the treaty will come into effect and no official publication gives any timeline or even rough dates.
What we know, is that the treaty text was due to be published in Autumn 2025, but has not yet been published.  Once the text is published there is still a ratification process that needs to be completed which may take some months.  From what we already know about the contents of the treaty some aspects require physical infrastructure to be built for example the much talked about “Schengen Shack” at the Gibraltar International Airport.  It is also anticipated that this sort of infrastructure may also take at least months to construct. 
A report by El Pais on 24 August 2025 that reported that the Gibraltar Brexit Treaty could come into effect by as early as January 2026, with passport controls disappearing at the border, is not considered by the GFSB to be accurate or reliable.  Certainly, as the autumn has progressed without the treaty being published, a January 2026 implementation date seems practically impossible. 
Therefore the answer to the question “when will the treaty come into effect” is that we do not know because there is no official published information on the subject.  We expect that a timetable for implementation will be published as soon as it is known.
