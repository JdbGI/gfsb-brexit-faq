HM Government of Gibraltar is delighted with the strong response to its recent call for public engagement on the agreement on a treaty with the United Kingdom and the European Union in respect of Gibraltar.


Already, many excellent and thoughtful questions have been submitted to our dedicated email address: UKEUTreaty@gibraltar.gov.gi


As we prepare our responses, we are committed to ensuring that these answers benefit the wider community. All replies will be published in the form of a public Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) document, addressing the key concerns raised by members of the public.


We will, of course, ensure that all published content is fully compliant with data protection requirements. No personal information will be shared.


This initiative reflects our ongoing commitment to transparency, clarity and public involvement during this historic process.


Please continue to send us your questions.


We are listening. We are ready to respond.


Email: UKEUTreaty@gibraltar.gov.gi


Include your full name and mobile number so that a member of the Government team can follow up directly.


Together, we will navigate this new chapter with confidence and unity.
