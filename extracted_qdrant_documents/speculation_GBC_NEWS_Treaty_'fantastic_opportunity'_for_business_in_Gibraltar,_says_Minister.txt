I think the treaty is an excellent platform. It interacts with businesses insofar as the transaction tax is concerned, but more importantly it provides something we have never had before: certainty in our relationship with Spain. The treaty establishes clarity as to what the border will look like and, in that sense, represents a reset in the relationship between business and the jurisdiction. From that perspective, the treaty is a fantastic opportunity for business in Gibraltar. Businesses can now plan ahead, with a clear understanding of what Gibraltar as a jurisdiction will look like. As a legal document, the treaty provides the certainty businesses need to plan for the next five to ten years.
