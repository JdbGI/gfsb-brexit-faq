### Slides – Customs Union Mechanics

* We have a customs union with the EU
* Not part of the EU Customs Union
* In that union: equivalence of rules and standards, and no duties on goods moving between unions

**How goods move**

* Import/export by land (some exceptions)
* EU goods may be imported by sea if cleared in Algeciras, then direct into Gibraltar port
* All goods and third-country goods can be exported by sea to non-EU countries
* All customs clearing (import/export) will be carried out at DCPs (Designated Customs Posts):

  * La Línea
  * Algeciras
  * Sagunto (near Valencia)

---

### Import

* Goods from for example Madrid/Paris move to DCP

* That move will be under a T2 transit procedure

* All customs formalities at DCP, save for EU customs formalities

* No EU VAT charged on goods going to Gibraltar

* T2 closed at DCP, then T2Gi opened

* Transit under NCTS IT system from DCP to Gibraltar

* Gibraltar Customs close T2Gi on arrival

* Holding area near customs gate

* At that moment, Gibraltar Customs levy transaction tax where goods are to be placed for sale in the Gibraltar market

* Transaction tax and excise only levied when goods are brought to Gibraltar to be sold in Gibraltar

* Special customs procedures recognised, including bonded warehouses

* If goods go into special procedures, TT/excise is not triggered

* Customs warehouse (bond): goods can be kept for 1–9 months (not for ship suppliers)

* Inward processing and temporary admission: Goods cab be kept for 3 months, extendable where justified

* EU goods can be kept in these special procedures – this is unique in the EU

---

### Chief Minister – Sea Imports

* Not agreed to direct import by sea because that would mean EU customs officers in Gibraltar port

* Solution: clear in Algeciras, then move by land to Gibraltar

* Bond limit 1–9 months

* In the EU, bonding does not exist for EU-produced goods, only for third countries

* Our regime allows EU goods to be kept for is less time than now, but more time than in the EU where EU goods cannot be bonded at all

### Export Gibraltar → EU

* Reverse procedure: T2Gi via NCTS to DCP

* At DCP, customs formalities for entry into EU

* DCP closes T2Gi and opens T2 to destination in EU

* Does not apply to ship suppliers (traditional supplies, bunker fuel, aircraft)

* Separate regime for non-EU goods Gibraltar → EU

* T1 / T1Gi transit procedures

---

### Non-EU Goods

* Both Gibraltar Customs and DCP responsible for authorisation under special procedures

* DCP levies EU external tariff duties

* Proceeds revert to Gibraltar

* Non-EU goods:

  * Customs warehouse: indefinite
  * Inward/temporary processing: up to 24 months

* If mixed bond, EU bond rules apply to all

* Export non-EU goods Gibraltar → EU:

  * T1Gi to DCP and cleared there
  * Non-EU goods may be exported directly by sea without physical presentation at DCP

### Independent Consultative Body

* Appointed by Gibraltar and Spain
* Role: assess effect of tax rates on trade between Gibraltar and EU/Campo
* Similar to existing Gibraltar–Germany mechanism
* Likely ally: although TT lower than Spanish IVA, goods often more expensive in Gibraltar
* Purpose: avoid significant distortions
* Can recommend higher or lower TT

  * Higher: up to level to remove distortion
  * Lower: up to 2% below EU VAT, but not below 15% (EU VAT Directive floor)
* Recommendations can be accepted or rejected
* Clause to trigger if non-compliance

---

### Excise

* Minimum EU excise duties apply on implementation
* Price differential mechanism: not more than 15% on retail price of cigarettes
* No excise on fuel in petrol stations (bunkering fully exempt from TT and excise)
* After 3 years: no lower than 6% of Spanish excise rates
* Consultative body also assesses competition distortion
* Deviation possible in certain circumstances

---

### Standards / Compliance

* From entry into force, all goods sold in Gibraltar must comply with EU standards
* See Technical Notice published yesterday
* Goods from EU presumed compliant (checked at DCP)
* Exceptions: some foods, medicines, medical devices imported for GHA and professionals
* Market surveillance body: OFT
* Business licences for manufacturing will require compliance with EU rules

### Personal Allowances

  * Equivalent limits for cigarettes and alcohol
